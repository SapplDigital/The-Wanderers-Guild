THE WANDERER'S GUILD - playtest build
=====================================

Thank you for playing. This is an early, short piece of a larger game: a cozy dark-fantasy
exploration game with turn-based fights. You look after a run-down adventurers' guild in a
small village at the edge of a forest.

Everything you see is unfinished: characters are simple shapes, the art and sounds are
placeholders. Please play the way you normally would. There is no wrong way to play it.


STARTING
--------
Double-click WanderersGuild.exe. (Windows may warn about an unknown publisher: choose
"More info" > "Run anyway".)

The start screen offers:
- New Game  - starts from the beginning. If there is already a saved game, it asks first.
              Your old save is only replaced when the game saves again.
- Continue  - only shown when there is a saved game; carries on where you saved.


SAVING AND QUITTING
-------------------
- F9 saves (keyboard). The game says "Saved." or "Can't save right now."
  Saving isn't possible during a fight, a conversation or a camera sequence.
- Closing the window also saves, if you're not in a fight or conversation.
- There is one save slot. There is no pause or quit menu: close the window to quit.


CONTROLS                      Keyboard / mouse                 Gamepad (Xbox layout)
--------                      ----------------                 ---------------------
Move                          W A S D or arrow keys            left stick
Run                           hold Shift                       hold B
Interact / talk               F                                A
Turn the camera               Q / E, or hold right mouse       right stick
                              button and drag
Tilt the camera               hold right mouse button and      right stick up/down
                              drag up/down
Zoom                          mouse wheel                      LB / RB
Save                          F9                               (close the window)

Menus and conversations: up/down (W/S, arrows, stick or D-pad) to choose, F / A to confirm,
Esc / B to go back or leave. The mouse works too.

Fights (turn-based): move the cursor with the mouse or W A S D, 1 / 2 / 3 pick an action,
F or left click confirms, Esc cancels, Space ends your turn.
Gamepad: A confirms, X / Y / D-pad up pick an action, B cancels, Start ends your turn.

The orange marks in the top left corner are your health.


WHAT TO DO
----------
Walk around, look at things, talk to people and go wherever you like. Play for as long
as it holds your interest and stop whenever you want.


WHEN YOU'RE DONE
----------------
Please fill in FEEDBACK.txt (next to this file) and send it back to the person who gave you
the game. Short answers are fine. Honest answers help most, including "I was bored" or
"I didn't get it".

If something broke, it helps to also send this file:
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\The Wanderer's Guild\logs\godot.log
(paste that path into the Windows Explorer address bar).


KNOWN LIMITATIONS
-----------------
- Placeholder graphics and sound; no music beyond a few short cues.
- No settings, pause or quit menu. Close the window to quit.
- No save button on the gamepad; closing the window saves.
- It's a small area and a short session.
