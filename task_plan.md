## Open risks
- Backlog: minimal inventory/item support (ItemData + carried items + compact HUD list). In slice scope (GDD 18.1 inventory-lite) but unscheduled; M9 keeps the ring and glint stone as flags.
- Combat UX backlog (playtest): "smart attack movement" (click a target out of reach → walk into range and attack). Not scheduled; not part of M8.
- Texel shimmer during fast rotation (not measurable in screenshots — check by hand).
- Distant ruin textures alias into noise at 100 m+; may need a lower-detail ruin material or mipmap bias.
- Controller: title menu and dialogue work with a pad (synthetic-event tests); never tried with a physical pad. No pause menu.
- Tree crowns have no collision, so the camera can clip into them in dense forest at LOOK zoom. Fix in polish with occluder fading (dither-fade meshes between camera and player).
- Camera pull-in behind roofed buildings: fixed for the guild in M15 (doorstep occlusion); still happens behind the three houses and the well roof.
- Guild: damaged-state detail is subtle at normal zoom; indoor LOOK sees through the cut-away far walls (dollhouse); no fire sound (bank has none; polish).
- Save: one slot; `--fresh` (or a new game) overwrites the save on the next F9 or window close. No gamepad save binding yet (no menus); autosave on close covers pad players. Title screen with New Game / Continue since M15.
- Test exits sometimes report leaked Ogg playback objects (audio still playing at quit; intermittent, 0 of 3 reruns). Harmless; clean up in polish if it gets noisy.
- Overlook reveal (M13): the return frame at FOV 40 can't hold the island's top/spire under the +10° pitch cap (the hold at FOV 55 can); the island reads flat and grey in the fog (art/fog, not camera). Entering the reveal box from the side slopes returns with the spring arm pulled in by the rising hill behind the player. `reveal` reuses the discovery sting until a dedicated one exists.
- Exploration HUD lists every active quest (M14). Long objectives wrap in the 192 px corner; revisit the layout if quests multiply.
